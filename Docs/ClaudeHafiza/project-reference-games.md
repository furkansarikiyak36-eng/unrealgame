Explicit design references (user, 2026-10-14). Each game gives one thing, nothing else:
- **Subnautica** — story told only through remnants in the world, no cinematics; simple but effective.
- **Frostpunk + The Long Dark** — every region offers hard but surmountable materials (equipment/difficulty like The Long Dark); each day you must make choices to reach the next day (Frostpunk). Every day enjoyable and hard.
- **Farming sims** — crops, livestock and what animals give (hides etc.).
- **7 Days to Die** — raids against the daily base; you can go underground; packing earth/concrete. Voxel kept very minimal, combined with raids. NOT full voxel digging.
- **Sons of the Forest** — every mechanic you can do with logs, stone and sticks; physical assembly.
- **Death Stranding** — graphics/story not taken; the world is truly hard, but once explored you can add bridges and ropes so it gets easier: civilising the world.

Other games (The Forest, Valheim, Vintage Story, Green Hell, Rust, Factorio, Minecraft) are only similarity comparisons, never attributed sources.

**Why:** web Claude's reports mis-attributed (e.g. excavation to 7 Days to Die as full voxel, Sons of the Forest as digging-only).

**How to apply:** attribute mechanics this way in docs/catalogs/proposals; judge priorities against these pillars. Recorded in `Docs/KARARLAR.md`. See [[project-greybox-rebuild]].
